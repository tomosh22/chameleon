# WP-40: bind-planner pairing defects (R-48, R-50; ADR-0043)

Branch `wp/40-planner-pairing`. Diagnostic range of ADR-0043: CML3600-3629 (planner). Used: **CML3600** (a guard cannot be
bound consistently with the statics of its block, error) and **CML3601** (a piece of a split aggregate, error). Everything else
ends in the existing CML3207 / CML3214 with the reason appended, as WP-37 did.

The rule of the package (ADR-0043, ADR-0042's principle): every defect ends in a correct pairing or a refusal with a planner
diagnostic, never in a silent wrong pairing or a silent re-initialisation. When in doubt, refuse.

## What was built

### R-48 item 1: statics of inlined lambdas (clang-cl `/O2 /Ob2`)

What clang records (clang 18 + lld, the fixture of WP-37 and this package's `planner_pairing_o2` at `-O2`, read with
`llvm-pdbutil dump --symbols`): when the call operator is inlined there is no procedure for it; its static `sname` is an
`S_LDATA32` of the **calling** procedure (`S_GPROC32 o2_step`, three `S_LDATA32 sname`, then the `S_INLINESITE`s), with nothing
that says which lambda it belongs to. No guard is recorded. The patch object still names the static after its lambda
(`?sname@?1???R<lambda_0>@?0??o2_step@@YAXXZ@QEBA@XZ@4UO2Name@@A`).

- `static_candidates` (data in a call operator): records of the name whose procedure is the lambda's enclosing function
  (`lambda_enclosing`: the display name without `::<lambda>::operator()::<name>`) are candidates too ("inlined records").
- They are never paired by name: pass A defers a group whose single candidate is such a record, and so does step (2) of the
  undefined-symbol chain.
- The closure step (`pair_statics_by_closure`): when the enclosing function has inlined records, nothing is paired by closure
  and nothing is declared new. The records directly in the function are first counted against the function's own statics of
  that name (the patch declares them there too); what is left over is an inlined lambda's. If the function has as many records
  of the name in its lambdas (own procedures and inlined) as the patch has statics, they are left to the section layout (pass B
  step 3, their RVA) and to their place in the function (step 4); otherwise the lambdas changed and nothing is placed by layout
  either (CML3207). Logged as `WP40-INLINED function='o2_step' statics=sname inlined=3 old=3 patch=3 placed-by=layout-or-place`.
- Under clang-cl the layout is known whenever another static of the translation unit was matched: the statics and their guards
  (pass C) are placed by it. Without a layout the by-place step refuses them (clang-cl's guards have no record), CML3207.
  CI (head `020866a`, windows-2022 clang-cl RelWithDebInfo, job 138909495815): `WP40-INLINED function='o2_step' statics=sname
  inlined=3 old=3 patch=3 placed-by=layout-or-place`, `RELOAD-OUTCOME applied`.
- cl at `/O2 /Ob2` (CI, same head, both windows-2022 cl cells) emits the call operators as well and records each lambda's
  statics twice, in its operator() and in the caller, at one address. The first push counted them twice ("6 there, some of
  them inlined, 3 in the patch") and refused; a record in the caller at the address of a record in a call operator is now
  the same variable, and the statics pair by closure.
- Locally (clang 18 + lld, `-O2`): fixture `lambda_statics` (WP-37) plans with every `sname` `compiland-static-rva` and every guard
  `compiland-layout` on the old guard next to it, `new=0`; on `main` all seven `sname` were `patch-new-data` with their guards on
  the old guards. Fixture `planner_pairing_o2`: the same for its three lambdas.

### The guard rule (R-48 items 1, 2, 6, 9; ADR-0043 points 1 and 2)

`settle_guards()` replaces pass C. A guard `?$TSS0@<scope>@4HA` (cl's `?$S1@<scope>@4IA` under `/Zc:threadSafeInit-`) belongs to the
statics declared in the same decorated scope, `?<name>@<scope>@4...` (read-only statics, which have no guard, are left out):

| Statics of the scope | Guard with an old record (cl) | Guard without a record (clang-cl) |
|---|---|---|
| one of them refused | settled with the error, no CML3211 (item 9) | the same |
| all new | **new** (its name matched an old guard of another static: cl numbers guards per function) | new, never placed by layout (item 1, 2) |
| none at all | stays bound | not placed by layout; CML3211 as before |
| some new, some bound | stays bound (the new one needs no guard, or its own guard has no record and is refused) | **CML3600**: which guard is whose cannot be told |
| all bound | stays bound | placed by layout only if the layout is known, the address lies in this compiland's contribution, in the image section the layout maps into (learned per object section), and no record of the compiland names other data there (item 2); otherwise **CML3600** (item 6: this was CML3211 and the old object was constructed again) |

A new defensive check: two statics of the patch paired with one old record are CML3207 (two groups can see the same record
since inlined records are candidates of the function's own statics too).

### R-48 item 3: a new file-scope static function is not the old `X::f`

The function fallback of `static_candidates` (no record with the exact display name) now requires the candidate's scope
(namespace and class) to be the member's scope, and drops a candidate whose address carries a public symbol of another
decorated name (an external function: statics have no `S_PUB32`). Function-local functions keep the old fallback (cl's nested
lambda spelling is not read). On `main`: `?helper@@YAHH@Z` was hooked onto `Foo::helper`.

### R-48 item 4: aggregates clang-cl splits (`name.N`)

A writable static whose name has a trailing `.<digits>` (`is_split_piece_name`: a decorated name or a C identifier) is accepted
only when a data record of the patch object names it, i.e. a SECREL relocation in its `.debug$S` targets it (CodeView records
no fragment). Otherwise **CML3601**. Verified locally: clang 18 `-O2` splits `namespace { int s_parts[3]; }` and the C
`static int s_last[3]` into pieces that no `.debug$S` relocation names, while `c_statics`'s one-field `inc_state.0` is named by
its record (and binds as before). Pairing pieces with old pieces was not possible: the old PDB has no record of them either.

### R-48 item 5: two lambdas that swap places (ADR-0043 rule (d), the orchestrator's ruling after hand-back 1)

1. **Different call operators.** Before pairing statics by closure number, the signature of the old call operator of that
   closure is compared with the patch's operator() of the same number (closure numbers aside); different: CML3207 for the
   statics. (WP-37's call-operator rule already refuses that reload with CML3214.)
2. **Indistinguishable call operators** (the same signature but for the closure's number; Godot's `SNAME`). The planner
   pairs their statics by number and reports them in `BindPlan::lambda_sets`. The pipeline then compares, with WP-25's
   unchanged-function comparator, each new call operator with each old one under the hypothesis "new lambda i is old lambda
   j" (the statics and guards of i are taken at the addresses the plan gave lambda j's): `pair_lambdas_by_code`
   (`hook_narrowing.cpp`). Pairs equivalent one-to-one are paired by that (also against the numbers); one lambda left on
   each side (its body changed) is paired with the other; otherwise, or where a body cannot be compared, the numbers stay and
   the warning **CML3602** names the function and the lambdas. Where the code says the lambdas changed places, the unit is
   planned again with `PlanInput::closure_pairing` (once): the statics, the cl guards (by their records), the call operators'
   hooks follow it, and clang-cl's record-less guards are placed where the layout puts the guard of the patch's lambda with
   the old number (the twins of the paired statics teach that layout). Logged as `WP40-LAMBDAS function='...' lambdas=N
   moved=N by_number=0|1`.
3. **String literals (the check asked for in the ruling).** The comparator does not compare read-only data by address: a
   fresh constant the patch carries (a string literal `??_C@...`) is compared by its bytes at the old target
   (`check_fresh_constant`), so `SNAME("alpha")` and `SNAME("beta")` are not equivalent. Verified with a unit test (same code,
   different literals, swapped: paired by the literal) and locally with clang 18 + lld on an `SNAME("alpha")` /
   `SNAME("beta")` function at `-O0` and `-O2 -fno-inline`: no swap (no pairing change, no warning), swap (both moved), swap
   with one literal edited (both moved, step 2), both edited (CML3602, numbers).

### R-48 items 7, 8, 10, 11 (the by-place step of WP-37)

- **7:** the guard scan skips the member itself and members that are guard variables (a pair of `$S1`, as the review
  described it, pairs by place). **But the shape does not occur with cl** (CI, head `020866a`, cl 19.44, both windows-2022 cl
  cells): with `/Zc:threadSafeInit-` cl writes ONE bitmask `?$S1@?4??pp_tsi@@YAHH@Z@4IA` for the function (in the scope of
  its first block), with no record in the PDB, in a COMDAT section of its own. Its old address cannot be found, so the two
  statics stay refused under cl (CML3207, "the guard variable 'pp_tsi::$S1' ... has no record in the old image"), which is
  the safe outcome and is kept. clang-cl writes one `$S1` likewise and places it by the section layout: paired (CI,
  windows-2022 clang-cl RelWithDebInfo, `planner_pairing` 22/22).
- **8, kept:** an unbound guard of a new sibling static in the block still refuses. Whether `$TSS2` belongs to the new static or
  to the old `names` (a new static declared in front takes the old one's number) cannot be told from the names; a wrong guess is
  an object never constructed. Seen with clang 18 on `planner_pairing/sibling.cpp`: the new `extra` takes `$TSS1`, the second
  `names` moves to `$TSS2`.
- **10:** the reason names the static whose layout prediction decided and the section of that static, and is appended to an
  earlier reason instead of replacing it.
- **11:** the record names are compared by their last component on both sides (cl writes ``"`opts'::`2'::names"`` for a
  function with internal linkage). Over-refusal fixed.

### R-50: the four WP-36 re-review nits

1. The function-start check of the prover looks at every relocation that starts less than 8 bytes before the function, and one
   of unknown width counts as reaching in.
2. `parse_layout` keeps `rex_seen` and a legacy SIMD prefix (66, F2, F3, F0): VEX/EVEX after any of them is not located.
3. The `lea` rule's reason is worded as what it protects (32-bit RVA arithmetic `lea r32,[index+sym@IMGREL]`), in
   `imgrel_site.h`, the prover's message, `test_imgrel.cpp` and `docs/wp/WP-36.md`. The rule is unchanged.
4. `pipeline.cpp` logs `WP36-IMGREL` at Info only for an object with image-relative sites.

(R-50's last sentence, elision of functions with a jump table in code, is R-46 and not this package.)

## After the review of PR #72 (head `34d7a6c`)

| Finding | Fix |
|---|---|
| 1 A bitmask `$S1` was tied to the statics of its own block only | `guard_covers`: a `$S<n>` guards every block of its function (one per function, in its first block's scope) |
| 2 Rule (d) compared stripped closure names with raw decorated names (`?A0x<hash>@`): lambdas in an anonymous namespace were never compared | every closure comparison and twin search uses the plain spelling (`plain_name`, `by_plain_name` in hook_narrowing) |
| 3 The "two statics on one old record" check keyed records, not addresses | keyed by RVA |
| 4 An own static of `f` counted against the records in `f` could take an inlined lambda's live record | pass A does not pair own statics of a function whose lambdas hold statics of that name; the counts include the own statics; only when every lambda static has a record in a call operator are the records in `f` the own ones |
| 5 Rule 3 paired left-over lambdas in order | by number; where that number's old lambda is taken by a code-equivalent one: **CML3603** (error) |
| 6 `overridden_closure_static_` written, never read | removed |

Consequence of 4 (over-refusal, safe): a function that has its own static `x` and gets a new lambda with a static `x` is
refused (CML3207), because the record of the own `x` cannot be told from an inlined lambda's.

## Harness change (outside the package's directories)

`tests/harness/src/{scenario.h,scenario.cpp,runner.cpp}`: a reload step's `expect_error` may name alternatives
(`"CML3214|CML3207"`), and a new key `allow_error` lets a reload succeed or be refused with one of the listed ids; the steps after
it must hold for both outcomes. Needed because the planner pairs under one compiler and refuses under the other (clang-cl splits
an aggregate that cl keeps whole; what cl records for an inlined lambda's static at `/O2 /Ob2` is not known), and the scenario
format has no step that depends on the compiler. About 50 lines; no existing step changes behaviour.

## What was learned

- clang 18 at `-O2` records the static of an inlined call operator in the calling procedure, before its inline sites, and emits
  no procedure for the operator(); the guard has no record (as always under clang-cl).
- clang numbers the guards of a function in declaration order across blocks (`$TSS0`, `$TSS1`, ...): a new guarded static in
  front of an old one takes the old one's guard name. cl does the same (per function, the fixture `lambda_statics` shows
  `$TSS0`/`$TSS1` in two blocks). A guard matched by its name can therefore be another static's.
- clang 18 with `-fno-threadsafe-statics` writes one `$S1` bitmask for a function (in the first block's scope), not one per block.
- SROA of a global: LLVM names the parts `<name>.<k>` in offset order and records no CodeView record for a fragment; a part that
  covers the whole variable keeps its record (fixture `c_statics`).

## Deferred, and why

- **Lambdas whose bodies all changed and that also swapped places** are paired by number with CML3602 (rule (d) step 3).
- **A clang-cl over-refusal (accepted for now):** a plain static added to the block of a guarded static gives CML3600 (the
  guard without a record cannot be told to be the old one's).
- **An old image whose aggregate was split and a patch that keeps it whole** (or the reverse): the old pieces have no record, the
  whole variable of the patch finds none and is new (ADR-0029's "no record: new"). Not detectable from the PDB.
- **Item 8** stays refused (above).
- **Item 6** was only reproduced on a synthetic object (a unit test); two tries with clang 18 kept the statics' order.
- **A thread-local guard placed by layout** (WP-37 "Found, not fixed" 6, CML3102) is not in R-48's list and was not changed.
- **Item 9** has no observable scenario step (a scenario cannot assert the absence of a warning); unit test only.
